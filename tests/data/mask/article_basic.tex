\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
Working from source keeps every cross reference intact, as $x_i$ and
\cite{knuth1984} show.  % 行尾注释：只吞它自己这一段
%% 连续的整行注释合并成一个块

survive the round trip.

\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:mask}
  m(x) = \sum_{i} b_i
\end{equation}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Prose environments stay in the stream.
  \item Their \texttt{\textbackslash begin} wrappers are preserved verbatim.
\end{itemize}
